# PRODUCT DESIGN CONSTITUTION

Version: 1.0

Status: ACTIVE

This document is the design source of truth for the Chytayko application.

All future UI, UX, visual, illustration and product design decisions must comply with this document.

If a new design contradicts this document, the new design is considered incorrect until explicitly approved.

---

# PRODUCT VISION

Chytayko is not a typical children's application.

Chytayko is a digital family reading space.

The product exists to help parents and children read stories together.

The application should feel closer to a premium children's book than to a mobile application.

Reading is always more important than interaction.

Stories are always more important than interface elements.

The product should support a family reading ritual.

---

# TARGET AUDIENCE

Primary audience:

* Children aged 3–6 years.

Secondary audience:

* Parents.

The product must satisfy both audiences simultaneously.

Children should feel curiosity, comfort and magic.

Parents should feel quality, calmness and trust.

---

# CORE DESIGN PRINCIPLES

## Story First

Stories are the center of the product.

All interface elements are secondary.

## Calm Before Fun

The product should create calmness before excitement.

Avoid overstimulation.

Avoid excessive visual noise.

Avoid aggressive engagement mechanics.

## Parent Approved

Parents should feel that the child is reading a book.

Parents should never feel that the child is consuming addictive digital content.

## Book > UI

Books are more important than buttons.

Books are more important than navigation.

Books are more important than decorative elements.

## Reading > Interaction

The application exists to support reading.

The application does not exist to maximize taps.

## Illustration > Decoration

Illustrations should create emotional value.

Decorations should never dominate illustrations.

---

# BRAND PHILOSOPHY

The product should feel like:

* A premium children's book.
* A family reading ritual.
* A magical story world.
* A bedtime reading experience.
* A calm and safe environment.

The product must never feel like:

* A game.
* A social network.
* A streaming service.
* A dashboard.
* A SaaS application.
* YouTube Kids.
* Duolingo.
* A gamified engagement platform.

---

# CHARACTER SYSTEM

## Teri

Role:

Story Companion.

Teri is a warm friend who loves stories.

Teri is not a mascot.

Teri is not a narrator.

Teri is not a tutorial guide.

Teri is not a product logo.

### Mandatory Presence

* Splash Screen

### Optional Presence

* Home Screen

### Avoid Presence

* Reader
* Story Details
* Library
* Favorites

Books should remain the primary heroes of the product.

---

# ILLUSTRATION SYSTEM

Approved style:

* Watercolor
* Premium children's publishing
* Scandinavian storybook
* Soft
* Warm
* Timeless
* Gentle
* Handcrafted feeling

Forbidden styles:

* Pixar
* Disney-inspired 3D
* Cartoon Network aesthetics
* Neon illustrations
* Gaming illustrations
* Flat corporate vectors
* Emoji-style characters
* Hyperactive children's content aesthetics

Illustrations should feel suitable for a printed children's book.

---

# COLOR SYSTEM

## Design Philosophy

Books provide visual accents.

Illustrations provide emotional accents.

The interface remains neutral.

## Background Colors

Primary Paper:

Warm Cream

Secondary Paper:

Soft Linen

## Typography Colors

Primary:

Warm Brown

Secondary:

Muted Brown

Tertiary:

Soft Taupe

## Color Rules

Avoid:

* Neon colors
* Highly saturated colors
* Gaming palettes
* Corporate palettes
* Bright call-to-action colors

Colors should support calm reading.

---

# TYPOGRAPHY SYSTEM

## Brand Typography

Book-inspired.

Elegant.

Warm.

Readable.

Suitable for children's publishing.

## Headings

Readable.

Semi-bold.

Warm.

Human.

## Reader Typography

Maximum readability.

Left aligned.

Comfortable line height.

Reading-first hierarchy.

Typography should feel like a printed book page.

---

# COMPONENT PRINCIPLES

## Buttons

Buttons are secondary.

Buttons must never dominate books.

Buttons must never dominate illustrations.

## Cards

Avoid dashboard cards.

Avoid SaaS-style cards.

Avoid analytics-style cards.

## Shadows

Minimal.

Soft.

Used only when necessary.

## Borders

Subtle.

Quiet.

Non-distracting.

# FUTURE DESIGN GOVERNANCE

Every future screen must be evaluated against:

* PRODUCT_DESIGN_CONSTITUTION.md

Questions:

1. Does the design support reading?
2. Does the design keep books as heroes?
3. Does the design remain calm?
4. Does the design feel parent-approved?
5. Does the design feel like part of the Chytayko universe?

If the answer to any question is "No", the design must be revised.
